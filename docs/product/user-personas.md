# User personas

These are target-user hypotheses to validate with interviews; they are not research findings about particular named individuals.

## P-01 Owner / organization administrator
Owns cash tied in stock, wants a trustworthy picture across branches, invites staff, sets policy and reviews margin/valuation. Needs dashboard, branch comparison, exports, role control and audit trail. Risk: bypasses process to “fix” quantities unless correction flow is fast.

## P-02 Operations / warehouse manager
Coordinates receiving, transfers, counts and fulfillment across sites. Needs queue/status views, stock discrepancies, assignment, scan-friendly workflows and traceability. Risk: branch staff have inconsistent SKU and UoM conventions.

## P-03 Buyer / procurement officer
Maintains suppliers, monitors reorder requirements, raises purchase orders and follows partial deliveries. Needs supplier history, expected dates, remaining PO quantities and reorder suggestions. Risk: forecast is distorted by open orders or wrong lead times.

## P-04 Sales representative
Quotes and enters customer orders, checks availability and communicates delivery/payment status. Needs customer lookup, price controls, order tracking and visibility scoped to assigned accounts/branch. Risk: promises stock reserved by another seller.

## P-05 Fulfillment / stock clerk
Receives, picks, transfers and counts physical products, often on phone/tablet with a barcode scanner. Needs large touch targets, quick SKU lookup, clear location and robust retry feedback. Risk: duplicate submit under weak connectivity.

## P-06 Finance / bookkeeper / auditor
Reviews invoices, receipts, supplier/customer balances and stock valuation; exports to accounting. Needs immutable references, period filters, corrections and role-limited access. Risk: operational status mistaken for accounting settlement.

## P-07 SaaS support/operator (internal)
Supports tenant onboarding, billing status and service health without broad access to customer data. Needs scoped diagnostics and auditable support access. Requires explicit customer-consented break-glass workflow; no routine cross-tenant data browsing.
